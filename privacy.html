Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AI TikTok Stack handles information when users connect and authorize their TikTok accounts.

1. Information We Access

AI TikTok Stack may access basic TikTok account information necessary to identify an authorized account and use approved TikTok publishing features.

2. TikTok Authorization

Access to TikTok is granted through TikTok's official authorization process. AI TikTok Stack does not require users to provide their TikTok password directly to the service.

3. How Information Is Used

Authorized information is used only to operate requested features, including publishing videos to the connected TikTok account.

4. Tokens and Credentials

Authentication tokens and credentials used to access connected services should be stored securely and are used only to perform authorized actions.

5. Third-Party Services

AI TikTok Stack may interact with third-party services including TikTok and AI content-generation providers. Those services operate under their own privacy policies.

6. Data Sharing

AI TikTok Stack does not sell personal information. Information is only shared with third-party services when necessary to provide requested functionality.

7. Revoking Access

Users may revoke TikTok access through TikTok account settings or by disconnecting the authorized integration.

8. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be reflected on this page.
